1. What we collect on this website

  • Booking details. When you book a Revenue Audit, our scheduling provider (Cal.com) collects your name, email, phone number, company and any notes you add. If you book from the calculator, the numbers you entered are passed along as notes so we can prepare for your call.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can reply and follow up.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider and browser may log standard request data (IP address, device and browser type, pages requested). The site stores a small flag in your browser session so the intro animation does not replay, and caches files on your device to load faster. We do not run advertising trackers.
  • Third-party resources. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation code from the jsDelivr network. Those providers receive your IP address when your browser requests them.

2. How we use it

  • To schedule and run your audit and reply to your questions.
  • To prepare a proposal and, if you become a customer, set up and run your service.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. If you become a customer: calls and texts handled by Ava

When Linta sets up Ava for your business, we process calls and text messages between your callers and Ava on your behalf. That can include caller phone numbers, call recordings, transcripts, summaries, appointment details and text messages. For that data, you are the business that decides how it is used and Linta acts as your service provider.

  • Ava tells callers they are speaking with an AI assistant. Where the law requires it, callers are told the call may be recorded or transcribed.
  • You are responsible for having a lawful basis and any required consent to text your customers, including consent rules for automated texts. We set up opt-out handling (for example, replying STOP) as part of text-back.
  • We use the data only to provide and improve your service and to meet legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the service (scheduling, hosting, telephony, text messaging, speech and AI processing, calendar and CRM integrations you ask us to connect), and when required by law. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.

5. How long we keep it

Website and audit-booking data: as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period after. Customer call and text data: for the length of your service and a short period afterwards, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires otherwise.
