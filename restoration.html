The Answered-Call Guarantee

In your first 30 days, Ava answers 100% of the inbound calls routed to her, 24/7, and attempts a text-back on every missed one. If a single call goes unanswered or unlogged, we work free until the system is fixed. If we cannot fix it within 60 days, you get every dollar back.

We guarantee the system. You keep the customers.

Answered means Ava picks up and the call appears in your dashboard with a transcript or summary. Covered calls are the calls routed to Ava under your plan: on Catch, after-hours, busy and missed calls; on Capture and Dominate, every call. Not covered: outages at your phone carrier or other telecom providers that we cannot control (we still send a text-back wherever we can), and calls that never reach Ava because forwarding was switched off.
