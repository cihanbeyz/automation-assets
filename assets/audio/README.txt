Before adding recordings, obtain explicit recording and website-publication consent from each speaker. Use a natural, conversational performance, and keep its turn order aligned with the matching niche JSON script. After all approved human recordings are in place, set CONFIG.humanAudioApproved = true in linta.js. Keep fictional company/caller details visibly identified as illustrative. Do not present a demo recording as a real client testimonial.
